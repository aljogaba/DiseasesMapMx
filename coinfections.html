PRRSV and PCV2 Coinfection Dashboard

Exploratory epidemiological dashboard for visualizing positivity, coinfection patterns, vaccination status, production stage, and regional distribution of PRRSV and PCV2 in swine farms.

Exploratory Analysis Module

This internal dashboard summarizes PRRSV and PCV2 molecular positivity at pool and farm level. The pool-level analysis describes detection frequency in biological samples, while the farm-level analysis avoids overestimating farms with multiple positive pools.

Filters can be applied by region, production function, farm type, sample origin, production stage, sampling site, diagnostic result, and vaccination status.
